# 16 — What Experienced Roblox Builders, Artists and UI Designers Say: DevForum Critique Distilled

> **Chapter goal:** turn hundreds of DevForum critique threads into rules the bot can apply to its own builds, UI, icons, thumbnails and renders *before* the user sees them. The test is simple: would the work survive a "Rate my build x/10" thread?
>
> **Sources:** a local mirror of the DevForum (`.sources/devforum/`). It holds the 2,000 most-liked **Art Design Support** threads, a partial **Building Support** set (287 threads), a partial **Creations Feedback** set (81 threads) and the relevant **Community Tutorials**. Highest-liked threads were read first. Everything here is **[community]** opinion unless marked **[official]**. Where a community claim conflicts with the engine reference, the official doc wins, and that is noted.
>
> **Extends, does not repeat:** `02-visual-design-lighting-building.md` (lighting tables, scale, materials, budgets), `03-ui-ux-design.md` (UI construction, safe areas, touch targets) and `09-3d-art-assets-and-characters.md` (Blender pipeline, textures, limits). Read those for the *how*. This chapter is about the *judgement*: what reviewers notice, in what order, and what fixes they ask for.
>
> **Dates matter.** Most of the highest-liked critique threads are from 2018–2021. Back then lighting was set with `Lighting.Technology` (ShadowMap / Future). That property is now **deprecated** **[official]**. Read "ShadowMap" in old threads as `LightingStyle = Soft` + `PrioritizeLightingQuality = true`, and "Future" as `Realistic` + `true` (see 02 §1). The *aesthetic* advice still holds. Some *engine* advice has changed: textures can now be larger than 1024 px (09 §TL;DR 4), and fonts can be loaded with `Font.fromName` / `Font.fromId`.

## 0. TL;DR rules for the bot

1. **"Empty" and "inconsistent" are the two most common criticisms of builds.** About a third of build-feedback threads raise each one. Before showing a build, fill dead floor and wall space with props that tell you what the place is for. Also check that every asset shares one style: one shading style, one material vocabulary, one level of detail. ([cafe build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-cafe-build/314583), [modern cafe exterior](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-modern-cafe-exterior/356543), [harsh critics](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thorough-and-harsh-critics-needed/245421))
2. **Build in passes: primary shapes → secondary → tertiary.** Skipping the secondary pass (frames, trims, supports, overhangs) and going straight to small props is the classic beginner tell. ([first cafe build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-my-first-cafe-build/461349), [plan your builds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/as-a-builder-how-do-you-plan-your-builds/187515))
3. **Always work from reference images.** A floor plan for buildings, photos for props. Nearly every top answer to "how do I get better" says *reference*. ([plan your builds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/as-a-builder-how-do-you-plan-your-builds/187515), [can't get good](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-cant-get-good-at-building/272809), [Ruski house](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-2-how-to-design-a-simple-house/330237))
4. **Break repetition.** No copy-pasted tree, window or house at the same size and rotation. Use at least 2–3 variants of every repeated asset (species, size, rotation, colour), and at least 3 different boundary shapes. ([low poly city](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-low-poly-city/532255), [opinions on this build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/opinions-on-this-build/267360), [lowpoly map](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rate-my-lowpoly-map/309243))
5. **Add imperfection on purpose.** Leaning lamps, sagging wires, boards of uneven length, dirt, cracks, rotated rubble. "Everything looks perfect, clean, safe" is a criticism, not praise. ([practice cafe](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-my-practice-of-a-cafe/504502), [post-apocalyptic](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-first-post-apocalyptic-theme-build/451821), [Greek building](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-greek-building/633266))
6. **Build on one grid increment and never switch mid-build.** 0.25 stud (or 0.2) for structure, with 0.05 offsets for trim. This removes z-fighting and hairline gaps. ([curvature guide](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/fp1s-guide-to-building-curvature-in-studio/635127), [building tutorial for all](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919))
7. **Beat "boxy" with depth, not part spam.** Inset windows, protruding sills and frames, bevels made from wedges and cylinders, varied heights and rooflines. Experts praise *fewer* parts used well. ([polycity](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/polycity-ideas-criticsm/249503), [plan your builds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/as-a-builder-how-do-you-plan-your-builds/187515))
8. **Lighting is the second most-praised and most-criticised element of builds, and the #1 problem in renders.** Never present default lighting. Warm key light with cool shadows is the safe start (numbers in §4 and in 02).
9. **Post-processing restraint.** Over-strong sun rays, thick fog, Blur and maxed contrast are named over and over as the beginner trend. Keep `BlurEffect` out of gameplay entirely. ([showcase tips](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-and-showcase-tips-update/639045), [better lighting](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/development-tips-better-lighting/580976), [realistic lighting tips](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tips-on-realistic-lighting/1261456))
10. **Lighting can't fix bad colour or material choices.** "Picking the right material, the right colour for your build is essential, that's not the lighting's job to recolor stuff." ([cartoon lighting](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/cartoon-lighting-critiquereview/253439))
11. **Use about 3 shades of each colour** and match part colours to terrain colours. This adds richness without adding parts. ([plan your builds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/as-a-builder-how-do-you-plan-your-builds/187515), [hotel exterior](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/opinions-on-this-hotel-exterior/311732))
12. **Free models get called out.** One recognisable toolbox chair makes reviewers distrust the whole build. Build every visible asset, or replace it. ([quick build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/please-rate-my-quick-build-x10/314554), [little office](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-little-office/326832))
13. **UI: the thing you want clicked must be the most visible thing.** In a shop that means price and Buy, not the X. Readability and contrast make up the #1 UI critique category (~46% of UI threads). ([feedback on my UI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-ui/381928), [currency UI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thoughts-on-in-game-currency-ui/221658))
14. **UI: commit to one shape language and one padding system.** Pick fully round or barely rounded; half-rounded looks unlucky. Every gap should be the same or deliberately different. Misalignment by even 1 px gets spotted. ([shop GUI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thoughts-on-my-shop-gui/246399), [UI design feedback](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/user-interface-design-feedback/247155), [mod call UI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-does-this-mod-call-ui-look/259322))
15. **UI: one theme colour plus neutrals.** Avoid primary-colour soup and bleached yellow on grey. Body text needs ≥ 4.5:1 contrast. ([pro UI designer](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/professional-ui-designer-raising-guides-101-chapter-1/476101), [what UI to use](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/opinions-on-what-ui-to-use/316960))
16. **Icons and logos: text must be readable at small size, and the genre must be obvious at a glance.** Reviewers' first comments are "blurry", "can't read it" and "doesn't look like a fighting game". ([recent logo](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-recent-logo/461817), [game logo blurry](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/opinionfeedback-on-new-game-logo/529185), [first logo ever](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-first-logo-ever/318790))
17. **Renders and thumbnails: one focal subject, posed with intent, lit with rim or 3-point light, placed by the rule of thirds, telling a moment from the game.** A T-posed avatar on a blurred background is the archetype of amateur work. ([improve my GFX](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/in-what-ways-as-a-gfx-artist-improve-my-gfxs/911505), [GFX course](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-gfx-course-part-1-basics-of-gfx-and-tips/991540), [screenshot tips](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-and-ideas-for-my-screenshot/427359))
18. **Never use other people's art, stock images as the main subject, famous creators, or copyrighted logos/fonts** in GFX, icons or thumbnails. Reviewers flag it, and moderation can act on it. ([GFX course](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-gfx-course-part-1-basics-of-gfx-and-tips/991540), [dragonball logo](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-does-my-dragonball-logo-look/327982))
19. **Judge from the player's camera, at several angles, at a mid graphics level, with UI on.** One hero shot at max graphics hides problems, and reviewers ask for more angles. ([quick build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/please-rate-my-quick-build-x10/314554), [rate my build 2 hours](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rate-my-build-2-hours-on-build/349927))
20. **Run the §11 critique checklist on every screenshot before showing the user.** Fix every "fail" first, then report what is still weak. Never call a build finished because it has no obvious bugs.

## 1. Method and how to read the numbers

**Corpus.** From the mirror I picked every thread whose title contains *feedback / rate / critique / thoughts / opinion / review / improve / "how does … look"*. That gave 856 threads (672 Art Design, 184 Building). I sorted them into four domains by title: **builds/models**, **UI**, **icons/thumbnails/logos**, and **GFX/renders**. Threads with fewer than 40 words of replies were dropped, leaving 151 build, 69 UI, 74 icon/logo and 74 GFX threads. I keyword-tagged the replies to count *how many threads raise each kind of critique at least once*. Then I read the highest-liked threads and replies by hand to pull out the actual advice.

**Caveats.** Counts are approximate: keyword matching overcounts ("shadow" in UI usually means drop shadows), so trust the **ranking**, not the percentages. One prolific reviewer writes many of the long build critiques and nearly always says "add props", which inflates "too empty". The more expert reviewers (Aotrou, Squidzyee, Zomebody, iacxx, theloudscream, stravant) raise emptiness too, alongside consistency and composition. The mirror lost many images and some tutorial bodies, so a few famous tutorials are cited only for their surviving text and replies. Creations Feedback in the mirror is mostly mega-threads and tool showcases with short praise replies, so most critique data comes from Building and Art Design feedback threads, which follow the same "post work, get critique" format.

## 3. Building craft: what experienced builders actually do

### 3.1 Workflow (consensus)

1. **Purpose first.** Decide whether this is a game map, a showcase or a prop, and what the player should *feel* ("happy → bright colours; darker colours → sensation of unknown/insecurity"). ([general guide](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/a-general-guide-on-how-to-build/221681))
2. **Reference.** "When I'm building, 98% I got an image/reference." Use multiple images of the same subject. For houses, start from a real floor plan, and check that it fits the lot, how many players must fit *comfortably*, and whether your furniture fits the rooms. ([plan your builds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/as-a-builder-how-do-you-plan-your-builds/187515), [Ruski house](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-2-how-to-design-a-simple-house/330237))
   - Fa1seP0sitive on Ruski's house tutorial: "the biggest downfall I see with houses in Roblox games is **awkward layout**, even if it technically works."
3. **Greybox / concept build.** Aotrou (126 likes): "I tend to plan out (greybox) my world building before getting into the nitty-gritty." CorrivalRhyme: "build an entire composition out of just a few big bulky parts, then just refine it." ([plan your builds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/as-a-builder-how-do-you-plan-your-builds/187515)) Sketching is optional: "you can use whatever works best for planning … even just writing up some notes … draw over my studio screenshots." ([Ruski map layout](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853))
4. **Primary → secondary → tertiary.** "It looks like you skipped your secondary shapes and went straight to your small shapes." ([first cafe build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-my-first-cafe-build/461349)) This is the same 60/30/10 hierarchy as 02 §0.11. The critique shows it is the specific step beginners skip.
5. **Subdivide when overwhelmed.** "Instead of trying to build a super detailed house. Start with a room… if the room is too much then go down to a single item in the room. Really focus on scale." (reteach, 55 likes, [plan your builds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/as-a-builder-how-do-you-plan-your-builds/187515))
6. **Detail pass with purpose.** "Signs, air conditioners, wires, and vents. They all help fill up empty space that leaves your build looking bland." ([plan your builds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/as-a-builder-how-do-you-plan-your-builds/187515)) Look at flat walls and "add things like pillars, structural support, or textures to break up the blandness." ([where did you learn](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/where-did-you-learn-how-to-build-on-roblox-studio/1318373))
7. **Life and motion.** Particles, beams, point lights, fireflies, floating or rotating tagged objects driven client-side. "In showcases I think animation and movement adds so much." ([showcase tips](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-and-showcase-tips-update/639045), [building tutorial for all](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919))
8. **Iterate on critique.** Accepted answers in feedback threads are usually the OP's update list: more tables, more tree spread, islands in the staff area, fewer filters. Plan for at least one revision round. ([cafe build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-cafe-build/314583))

### 3.3 Variation and imperfection

- **Trees:** "the trunk is too straight … it starts thick and gets smaller", "leaves are too uniform and symmetrical", "make sure the different colour leaves are dotted around and not clumped together" ([thoughts of my tree](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thoughts-of-my-tree/239231)). Build the **skeleton** (trunk plus up to 3 main branches) first. Then add 2–3 leaf clusters rather than one blob, and add roots so the tree looks "welded" to the ground ([rate the tree](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rate-the-tree-i-made/257785)). The classic CSG method: one leaf blob union, duplicated, rotated and resized around the branches, with a dummy character next to it for scale ([trees CSG](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/efficiently-building-good-looking-trees-with-csg/23976)). Modern advice: move leaf blobs to a **MeshPart**. "We've replaced the CSG trees in Deathrun with these Meshes and saw a great performance improvement." (same thread)
- **Placement:** "Mix up the trees … rotate them around, making some taller, and some bigger. Maybe even using different trees." ([ski resort](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-ski-resort/289290)) "More variants (species)" is better than rotating one model ([lowpoly map](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rate-my-lowpoly-map/309243), [modern homestore](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-modern-homestore/337170)).
- **Boundaries:** "The same shape repeated over and over throughout the boundary of the map is quite boring … I'd say 3 [shapes] minimum." ([opinions on this build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/opinions-on-this-build/267360))
- **Buildings:** "all of the stores are the same just different colors. Try adding in different size stores." ([first build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-my-first-build/548831)) "Make other types of windows instead of placing the same ones on the other buildings … some of the windows don't look centered." ([low poly city](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-low-poly-city/532255))
- **Imperfection for realism:** "it's important when going for realism that things aren't too uniform especially when close together" (leaning street lamps, wires curved differently or lying on the ground) ([post-apocalyptic](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-first-post-apocalyptic-theme-build/451821)). "The boards are all uniform. Look at an actual wooden deck … different lengths, some have holes" ([practice cafe](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-my-practice-of-a-cafe/504502)). For rubble, "consider adding different shapes or possibly rotation … they seem to follow similar designs" ([Greek building](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-greek-building/633266)).
- **But keep modern/sci-fi orderly.** Lights in a research facility should be evenly spaced: "scientific buildings … are typically made with close attention to mathematics in their placement." Medieval or organic builds can be looser ([symmetry](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/for-builders-how-important-is-symmetry-to-you/129335)). See §10 for the symmetry debate.
- **Avoid the "grid-y" look.** "Too many builders make everything perfectly aligned on the XYZ axis and it results in entire maps … feeling 'grid-y' and overly designed." ([showcase tips](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-and-showcase-tips-update/639045), reply) Note: this is about *placement* of props and foliage. Structural parts should still sit on the grid (§3.4).

### 3.4 Precision: grids, gaps, curves, stairs

- **Grid increment.** fourpapa1: "THE GOLDEN INCREMENT IS 0.25 … 0.25 adds up to 0.5, which adds up to 1." By contrast, 0.1 "can add up to any number in between", which is where z-fighting and tiny gaps come from ([curvature guide](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/fp1s-guide-to-building-curvature-in-studio/635127)). Crykee: build on 0.2 or 0.25, and for detail that sticks out use 0.04 (on a 0.2 grid) or 0.05 (on a 0.25 grid). "Never switch between these increments in the middle of the build" ([building tutorial for all](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919)). Disagreement in §10.
- **Z-fighting fix:** don't nudge parts apart, because that leaves a visible line. Build on the grid and use more shape types instead: wedges and corner wedges for curves and ramps, cylinders for rounded edges, spheres for rounded corners ([building tutorial for all](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919)).
- **Small detail:** model it at 10× size on a 1-stud grid, then scale down. Sub-stud moves are not exact, and the error spoils solid modelling ([tips and tricks](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tips-and-tricks-guide/20964), [plan your builds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/as-a-builder-how-do-you-plan-your-builds/187515) "build it big and then resize it to small").
- **Curves:** wedge stair-stepping (5×5, 6×4, 7×3, 8×2) is the beginner method. It "can work against you later". Better: generate the arc with Archimedes, then "build from" it, duplicating road parts to make the matching sidewalk so the curvature matches exactly ([curvature guide](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/fp1s-guide-to-building-curvature-in-studio/635127)). GapFill (stravant) is the one plugin a sci-fi builder "can't live without" ([sci-fi guide](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-ultimate-guide-to-sci-fimodern-building/916024)).
- **Stairs:** step rise **≤ 0.8 stud**. At 1 stud the default rig sometimes reads a "climb" and hops. Better still: turn off collision on the visual steps and add an invisible ramp part along the slope (Aotrou, 41 likes, [let's talk stairs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/lets-talk-stairs/399364)). Check railing height against a dummy. This adds to the step and door numbers in 02 §0.9.
- **Scale against the avatar.** Sinks "rather high in comparison of your character" ([bathroom](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-can-i-get-some-feedback-on-this-bathroom-i-built/502468)). "Make the second floor higher as the windows looked like it's being squeezed … first floor windows are too high" ([low poly motel](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-low-poly-cafe-motel/319269)). Door frame the same size as a window ([harsh critics](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thorough-and-harsh-critics-needed/245421)). Keep an R15 dummy in every build scene.

### 3.5 Shape language and depth

- **Depth beats flat colour.** "Adding depth and simplified shapes … the depth of the window and the protrusion of the base from the face of the wall along with the details of the cage and window frame" ([polycity](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/polycity-ideas-criticsm/249503)). Overhangs should be wedges or negated unions, not draped single-colour parts that "look like cloth" ([low poly motel](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-low-poly-cafe-motel/319269)).
- **Height variation.** "Different heights give direction and are inherently more aesthetic" ([little office](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-little-office/326832)). "The building itself seems very… flat. Maybe some more drastic height differences" ([practice cafe](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-my-practice-of-a-cafe/504502)). "Not have the map on one flat surface. Have raised areas and low areas" ([plan your builds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/as-a-builder-how-do-you-plan-your-builds/187515)).
- **Real text and logos as parts** give a cartoon build a more solid look than decals. Keep decals for complex logos ([low poly motel](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-low-poly-cafe-motel/319269)).
- **Shape-language consistency.** "The entire map is composed of rigid shapes, but the smoke particle is round. It doesn't fit." "Why is the water neon??" ([harsh critics](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thorough-and-harsh-critics-needed/245421)). Particles, water and UI should share the world's shape language.
- **Even level of detail across objects.** "Make sure your main desk reflects the same level of detail as the rest of your build" ([little office](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-little-office/326832)). "It seems like you didn't really put effort on the chairs as you did on the bed" ([quick build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/please-rate-my-quick-build-x10/314554)). "Chair … is not as detailed as the rest of the build" ([Chinese house](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-chinese-styled-house/452433)). The least-detailed object sets the perceived quality.

### 3.6 Layout and flow (full treatment in chapter 10)

- **Flow:** players take the path that is straight ahead or widest, has fewer obstacles, or is brighter. Guide them with geometry, light, high-contrast lines and landmarks ([FPS map design](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/first-person-shooter-map-design-in-roblox-picture-heavy/25475)). In open worlds, a compact map of unique areas beats a huge repetitive one, and every POI needs a tall, distinct landmark ([Ruski open world](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-5-how-to-design-an-open-world-map/3554962)).
- **Centrepiece:** the tallest structure should be the finished centrepiece ([lowpoly map](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rate-my-lowpoly-map/309243)), and a grand room gets a centre feature ([cafe build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-cafe-build/314583)).
- **Symmetry as a tool:** "a symmetrical building in an asymmetric surrounding … an eerie obelisk in the middle. People would gravitate towards the obelisk because it looks important" (Aotrou). Symmetry reads calm and safe (roleplay); asymmetry reads uneasy (horror) ([symmetry](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/for-builders-how-important-is-symmetry-to-you/129335)).

### 3.7 Performance judgements reviewers make

- "2,500 parts can become a problem … if the player has not that good of a device" ([spaceship](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-spaceship/465491)). Reviewers flag high part counts, but the real cost depends on what the parts are. Anchored, opaque parts are cheap. Unanchored or colliding parts, transparency and textures are expensive ([large part count](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/large-part-count-how-to-defeat-lag/134232), Ravenshield, 37 likes).
- "The biggest memory hog is very often textures" (same thread). Use built-in materials where they work (02 §0.16).
- **Unions:** each *unique* union must be downloaded and holds child data for un-unioning. Duplicating one union is cheap, and thousands of unique ones are not ([unions lag](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/do-unions-cause-server-lag/84734), Zomebody, 83 likes; [trees CSG](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/efficiently-building-good-looking-trees-with-csg/23976)). Unions mostly cost the **client** (load, render, physics), not the server ([unions lag](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/do-unions-cause-server-lag/84734)). For something repeated across a map, like a fluted Roman column, make a mesh in Blender with Array + Boolean modifiers ([Roman statue](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-this-roman-statue/336412)).
- **Diagnose by looking around:** "Shift+F2 and Ctrl+F6 … too many triangles are drawn when looking in that direction" (the castle) ([reduce lag](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-do-you-actually-reduce-lag-on-already-laggy-places/112471), 43 likes). Modern equivalents: the MicroProfiler and render stats (chapter 12).
- **"Fewer parts is the real skill."** "If there is a way to make the same build with less parts, then kudos to you: because *that is the real challenge*." Tips: about 3 shades per colour, blend parts into smooth terrain by matching colours, and use clean CFraming with cartoon textures (nineteen85, [plan your builds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/as-a-builder-how-do-you-plan-your-builds/187515)).
- **Foliage density vs lag:** when a forest is "barren" but more trees would lag, use **grass/ground-cover meshes in variety** and props (logs, hay, paths) rather than more trees ([map so far](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thoughts-on-my-map-so-far/365106)).

\* **Official correction:** Glitterkittyparty108 says `ColorShift_Bottom` is the sunlight colour and `ColorShift_Top` "does nothing". Start from 02's values and raise them only if a screenshot looks flat.

### 4.2 Lighting rules distilled from critique

1. **Post-FX restraint is the most repeated lighting advice.** AerialsAbove (owner of Roblox Architects, reviewing new builders' showcases): "DO NOT over do the contrast, fog, or most importantly the sun-rays and, exposure, and brightness. This has become a very common mistake most new showcases are all making now as part of some bizarre trend." ([showcase tips](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-and-showcase-tips-update/639045)) Also: "Often I see people setting the sun rays to a ridiculously high level and it ends up distracting from the actual build" ([better lighting](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/development-tips-better-lighting/580976)). "The sun rays … are quite overpowering and they're downright opaque" ([café build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-caf%C3%A9-build/460811)). "Slow down on the fog a little bit … it looks really hard to see through" ([rate my build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rate-my-build-x10/307800)).
2. **Blur is for UI moments only.** "Blur is the stupidest thing and is only useful for user interface … For a high quality map, go for depth of field but make it very light" ([realistic lighting tips](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tips-on-realistic-lighting/1261456)). "Lower blurriness" is a standard fix ([post-apocalyptic](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-first-post-apocalyptic-theme-build/451821)).
3. **Visible light fixtures must emit.** "The airport is bright but **there's no light**" ([airport](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thoughts-on-my-airport/347308)). Lamps should cast light, and windows should read bright with bloom around them ([little office](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-little-office/326832)). Add uplights, walkway lights and wall lights for night scenes ([modern homestore](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-modern-homestore/337170)).
4. **Fix local dark spots.** "A bit dark at one part of the floor" ([opinions on this](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/opinions-on-this/302707)). "On the first image it looks a little dark … add some more street lights" ([rate my build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rate-my-build-x10/307800)). "Define the shelves in that dark space. Play with lighting and colors there" ([little office](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-little-office/326832)).
5. **Lighting supports design and doesn't replace it.** "If you want 'cartoony' you have to have a cartoony building style, then you can start building a lighting … particles, beams etc… is also part of the lighting building process" (Sofloann, [cartoon lighting](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/cartoon-lighting-critiquereview/253439)).
6. **Match genre.** Horror: dim overall, light only important areas. Simulators: bright but not overbearing ([realistic lighting tips](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tips-on-realistic-lighting/1261456)). Desert: sandy fog, orange-ish OutdoorAmbient, dark red Ambient, matching skybox ([building tutorial for all](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919)).
7. **The skybox is part of the lighting.** Pick a real-photo sky that matches the time of day ("afternoon sky"), and match the fog colour to it ([shadowmap tutorial](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805), [cartoon lighting](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/cartoon-lighting-critiquereview/253439)). For stylised games, a sky built from gradient parts plus fog needs no skybox at all ([cool sky](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/cool-sky-system-for-our-project/242166)).
8. **Day/night cycles are where lighting quality usually collapses.** Animate Brightness, ambient and colour with the clock ([day/night](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/a-guide-to-a-better-looking-and-more-realistic-daynight-cycle/392643)).

## 5. Colour and palette

- **Three shades per colour** across a build give richness without new geometry ([plan your builds](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/as-a-builder-how-do-you-plan-your-builds/187515)). "You only use 1 grass color, which is a bit bland … multiple grass colors" ([hotel exterior](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/opinions-on-this-hotel-exterior/311732)).
- **Homogeneous colour over large open areas gets dull fast.** You need "objects and different colours along the way to attract interest (differing shades of green, brown, grey)". Rocks that differ in colour from the cliffs they sit beside look "silly" ([is this place looking good](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/is-this-place-looking-good/281831)).
- **Colour temperature has meaning.** A green rocket exhaust reads wrong unless it is deliberately "futuristic plasma". Warm = fire, exhaust, home. Cool = tech, cold, night ([spaceship](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-spaceship/465491)). Cool shadows with warm light is the safe default ([hyper realistic](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/hyper-realistic-lighting-settings/915802), [cartoon lighting](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/cartoon-lighting-critiquereview/253439)).
- **Don't drown a build in one hue.** "Looks a bit too 'goldy'" ([resort](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thoughts-on-our-resort-build/382794)). "The entire build has a spectrum of colors between yellow and green. Could you possibly expand the spectrum to red" ([Chinese house](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-chinese-styled-house/452433)).
- **Grounded vs pastel.** "Props to not using flat tone lighting and overly pastel colors. Grounded lighting and colors brings more of an ambience, and in general seems to be more tolerable to play in for a longer time" ([low poly designs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/low-poly-designs/132229)). Others push *for* pastels in UI (§7). These two contexts differ: pastels suit UI chrome, while grounded tones suit long-play worlds.
- **Saturated neon on black is a known eyesore.** "No one likes to see bright neon on solid black" ([where did you learn](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/where-did-you-learn-how-to-build-on-roblox-studio/1318373)).
- **Tools named:** flatuicolors.com, Coolors and a colour wheel ([shop GUI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thoughts-on-my-shop-gui/246399), [drawn thumbnail](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-drawn-thumbnail/563796)). The bot should generate palettes in code (09 §TL;DR 15) and save them in the design doc.

## 6. Modelling, UV and texture tips from Q&A threads

(The full pipeline is in chapter 09. These are the corrections experts make over and over.)

- **"Low poly" is a technique, not a style.** "Low poly isn't a style! It's a method of creating meshes on which to bake a high poly model's texture. You're referring to textureless/minimalism/flat shading." (Aotrou, [lowpoly map](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rate-my-lowpoly-map/309243). Echoed by Intended_Pun in [low poly designs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/low-poly-designs/132229): "there is a common misconception that low-poly = more cartoony"). When writing a style spec, say "flat-shaded, untextured, small palette", not "low poly".
- **Never mix smooth-shaded and flat-shaded models in one scene.** This was the headline issue in the most thorough critique in the corpus ([harsh critics](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thorough-and-harsh-critics-needed/245421), FileSaved, 12 likes, "INCONSISTENT STYLES"). Set shading per asset in Blender deliberately.
- **Grass that doesn't match.** "The grass feels out of place and inconsistent with the plastic low poly vibe" ([low poly w/ terrain](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-low-poly-w-low-poly-terrain/450215)). If the style is flat or plastic, turn off terrain decoration grass, or tint it to match.
- **Smooth terrain with flat-shaded assets:** a known working combination. See §10 for the debate.
- **OBJ vs FBX:** "Most of the replies here suggesting to export it as an .fbx is saying so for the wrong reasons … for general background assets, .objs are typically better … If you aren't using a texture, use BrickColor properties" (Aotrou, [export coloured model](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-do-i-export-a-coloured-model-from-blender/276205)). Chapter 09 now prefers glTF/GLB. The lasting point: vertex or material colours don't reliably survive import. Either use a texture (a palette atlas) or split by colour and set `Color` in Studio.
- **Normal-map era note:** in 2019, before `SurfaceAppearance` existed, modellers baked AO from a high-poly normal map into the diffuse to fake detail. The advice was to keep the low-poly under 10k triangles and light the Blender bake scene like the Roblox scene ([normal maps](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-and-normal-maps-a-guide-to-baking-and-hacks-for-3d-modeling/371238)). Today, use real PBR maps (09 §4). The "bake AO into colour" trick is still useful for the flat style.
- **Normal map format:** Roblox uses **OpenGL (Y+)**. "If there are 2 normal maps named something like 'Normal_GL & Normal_DX', use the Normal_GL one" ([textures guide](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-texturesmaterials/1658926)). Metalness maps should be close to 0 or 1, and roughness can vary from 0 to 1 (same).
- **PNG over JPEG** for anything with edges, text or UI. "Please never use JPEG for anything outside of a few very limited use cases … your textures will become fuzzy" (Elttob, [JPEG or PNG](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/optimization-question-jpeg-or-png/241746)). (Another reply says it makes no difference after Roblox compression. See §10.)
- **Images look sharpest at their native size or at halvings of it.** "It's going to look blurry regardless of resolution unless you display it at the exact size that you uploaded it in" ([decals blur](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/prevent-large-decals-from-blurring/191925)). For UI images, design at the pixel size they will appear at (×2 for high-DPI). Run PNGs through **pixelfix** so transparent pixels don't create dark fringes ([high quality GUIs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-high-quality-guis/281602)).
- **Want the pre-2022 materials back?** Use `MaterialService` → `Use2022Materials` (property toggle), or MaterialVariants built from Roblox's published asset IDs ([old textures](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-do-i-get-back-old-textures/1920336), 66 likes). Pick one material generation per game, because mixing old and new looks inconsistent.
- **Bump maps on cartoon renders** ruin the cartoon feel. Sharp-edged bump on a humanoid looks wrong ([lukacors](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rate-my-gfx-lukacors/199629), [first ever GFX](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/my-first-ever-gfx/671075)).

## 7. UI design: process and the critiques that recur

(How to build UI is in chapter 03. These are the judgements reviewers make.)

### 7.1 Process top designers describe
1. **Questions first.** What are you making, why, and who is it for? "Always keep the UI simple for everyone." Prototype, then **watch someone use it**: "If the kid can't get it on the first try, I've failed as a designer. Sometimes you'll spot them moving to look at the wrong thing" (boatbomber reply, [pro UI designer](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/professional-ui-designer-raising-guides-101-chapter-1/476101)).
2. **Mood-board → vector mock-up → export → build.** Gather references (Pinterest "UI design"), design in a vector tool (Figma free; Adobe XD/Illustrator; Affinity Designer as a one-time-purchase favourite), export assets, clean them with pixelfix, and import. Principles: usable, navigable ("don't make it a maze just to find where the store is"), desirable ("clean with no excess materials") ([plan UI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-plan-design-and-build-quality-user-interfaces/281785), [UI fundamentals](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ui-design-and-the-fundamentals/1455805), [high quality GUIs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-high-quality-guis/281602)). **For the bot:** the user wants native editable instances (CLAUDE.md), so the mock-up stage becomes a Luau or `.model.json` layout plus a screenshot, not a flattened image.
3. **Hide secondary info.** "Make interfaces less cluttered by putting less important and or extra elements behind links, buttons, or other menus" ([UI fundamentals](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ui-design-and-the-fundamentals/1455805)).
4. **Combine three skills:** graphic art, a sense of space, and enough Luau to animate. "One ImageButton could change the entire profitability or efficiency of a game" ([high quality GUIs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-high-quality-guis/281602)).

### 7.2 Recurring UI rules from critique

- **Hierarchy matches intent.** In a potion shop the price must dominate: "if you look at your UI from far away, the thing that catches my eye the most is the X … your most dominant aspect is the X when it should be the item and price" (theloudscream, [feedback on my UI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-ui/381928)). "Which one is the primary button?" is a fail ([pro UI designer](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/professional-ui-designer-raising-guides-101-chapter-1/476101)). On a menu, the game title must not look like a button: "the largest/most obvious button is the Play button" ([UI screen](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thoughts-on-this-ui-screen/369362)).
- **Information first.** "It ostensibly looks very good, but you forgot the most important part of the UI: showing information! Your text is generally too small and also way too low contrast … a lot of players play primarily on mobile" (stravant, [first reaction](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-want-you-to-give-me-your-first-reaction-user-interface-for-a-roblox-game/383545)).
- **Commit to a corner radius.** "You seem to have hit the unlucky spot of fully round and not rounded … You've either got to commit to fully round or barely rounded" (iacxx, [shop GUI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thoughts-on-my-shop-gui/246399)). "If you are going to be using hard edges instead of rounded, then you should be sticking with those consistent shapes" ([feedback on my UI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-ui/381928)).
- **Consistent padding; don't cramp.** "The outline around the button/text is way too cramped … Space things out a little bit, don't clamp everything together" ([UI design feedback](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/user-interface-design-feedback/247155)). "The padding for your frames and text are inconsistent" ([feedback on my UI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-ui/381928)). Related items should be grouped into one element, not left as two floating ones with odd spacing ([please give feedback](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/please-give-me-feedback-on-my-ui/193278)).
- **Centre and align exactly.** Labels "aren't centered", "the blue button is not centered", "the text box and send button don't line up. Consider moving one of them over by 1 pixel" ([UI design feedback](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/user-interface-design-feedback/247155), [shop UI look](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/any-thoughts-of-feedback-on-my-shop-ui/234643), [mod call UI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-does-this-mod-call-ui-look/259322)). Use `AnchorPoint` 0.5,0.5 with Scale 0.5,0.5 to centre, and anchor corner buttons with AnchorPoint 1,0 at `{1,-8},{0,8}`. "Try to rely less on the UI editor" ([scale same on all devices](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-scale-or-position-same-on-all-devices/257721), 282 likes).
- **One theme colour.** "All UI should be using one theme color … Using more than one will create unevenness." Avoid low-contrast pairs (yellow on white, blue on black). Dark mode: base `#121212`, raise layers with white overlays, body text ≥ **4.5:1** ([pro UI designer](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/professional-ui-designer-raising-guides-101-chapter-1/476101)). "Avoid primary colors. They overwhelm the user … lean towards pastels … a large percentage of males are colorblind to some degree" ([what UI to use](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/opinions-on-what-ui-to-use/316960)).
- **Avoid the "cheap shop" look.** "The bright yellow and green makes it feel like it's one of those cheap mobile game microtransaction shops", but an all-grey version "looks a bit dead and not an inviting place to buy currency" ([currency UI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thoughts-on-in-game-currency-ui/221658), 28 likes). Aim between them: neutral panels, one inviting accent on the purchase action, and price text that contrasts with its background.
- **Essentials:** every shop needs a close button and a title, plus names and prices on items ([shop UI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-shop-ui/230138), [shop UI look](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-does-my-shop-ui-look/229952)).
- **Icons in a set must match:** "both the Shop and inventory one has no colours aside from black and white, but the spectate button has red and blue … the shop icon … looks very stretched … [one icon has an] outline … all the other images have no outline" ([sidebar](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rate-my-sidebar-gui/352064)). Sources named for consistent sets: Lucide (open source) and Fluency (2k icons) ([simple modern UI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-simple-modern-ui/1937876), [sleek UI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/heres-how-to-create-a-modern-sleek-ui-design-ui-tutorial/2528850)). Roblox also ships built-in vector icons (check chapter 03 and the docs before using them).
- **Fonts match each other.** "The Yes and No button text should have the same font with the message font … shapes should be rectangular to go with the text font" ([UI look better](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/need-help-making-ui-look-better/250407)). Drop shadows should be clearly visible or absent: "either make the ui flat or make the shadow bigger" (same thread).
- **Font freshness (2025).** "Fredoka One is so overused nowadays and some people tend to associate it with slop Simulator/Line/Obby/Tycoon games" ([game UI 2025](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-game-ui/3435304)). See §10 vs chapter 03.
- **Motion.** Pop-ups should scale from the **centre** (AnchorPoint 0.5,0.5), not from the bottom ([game UI 2025](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-game-ui/3435304)). "Animations can be faster if you want players to feel more responsiveness … start [the open] tween at like 70–80% of its final size rather than starting really small" ([UI anim](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/opinion-on-my-ui-and-what-to-add/341070)).
- **Don't waste space.** "The title bars … are way too huge and aren't contributing anything meaningful … We can barely see two rows of the inventory" ([UI anim](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/opinion-on-my-ui-and-what-to-add/341070)). A large pure-white background "is pretty harsh to look at" (same thread).
- **Tutorials should feel like events:** an essential tip in a weak bubble, with a Next button that looks like background, fails. Use short animation, possibly a mascot, and make the Next action primary ([tutorial UI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/constructive-criticism-on-tutorial-ui/269093)).
- **Image-based text breaks localisation.** "Since Roblox doesn't natively support the font you use, you'd have to use images, which are much harder to use when dealing with multiple languages" (Elttob, [first reaction](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-want-you-to-give-me-your-first-reaction-user-interface-for-a-roblox-game/383545)). This is another reason for native TextLabels (CLAUDE.md preference). Old sprite-sheet font hacks ([custom fonts](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/is-there-a-way-i-can-use-custom-fonts-in-studio/374818)) are obsolete now that `Font` objects exist.

## 8. Icons, thumbnails, logos and GFX: what makes them clickable

The platform specs (512×512 icon; 16:9 1920×1080 thumbnails; a custom thumbnail is required for Home recommendations as of 2026-10; thumbnail personalization) are in chapter 07 §7.11 and the announcement threads ([16:9 on Home](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/169-experience-thumbnails-are-coming-to-home-in-april-%E2%80%93-update-yours-now/2875237), [search shows 16:9](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/search-results-will-now-show-169-thumbnails/4525356), [custom thumbnail required](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/homepage-recommendation-eligibility-now-requires-a-custom-thumbnail/4945086), [personalization](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/live-now-personalize-your-thumbnails-to-attract-more-users/3257233)). This section covers craft.

### 8.1 Logos (game title art)
- **Process (PumpkinWhite, 425 likes):** "a game logo is the most important graphic when it comes to promotional materials." Treat it as thinking and strategy, not production. **Draft first**, even badly ("the objective of drafting is just drawing out your thoughts"), then build in vector ([logo design](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/basics-of-game-logo-design-process-and-base-structuring-tutorial-jan9-update/187522)).
- **Colour encodes genre.** Use colours that "give the players an understanding of the game's genre". Pick the palette from the topic's contents ("Planting Time": greens, dirt browns, flower accents) ([logo colors](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-game-logos-colors-beginners-guide/547033)).
- **The logo must say what you do.** "It doesn't really look like it'd be a fighting game … maybe include some people standing on the logo … with weapons" ([recent logo](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-recent-logo/461817)). "You also want to inform the player of how it may be like to play by just having them look at the logo … Another thing is to exaggerate it" ([Redshift Arena](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/redshift-arena-reloaded-logo-design-looking-for-feedback/334786)).
- **Restraint inside letters.** Stripes in the letterforms are "really distracting … their eyes start to get confused", and "there is a lot of red" ([logo 472603](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-a-logo/472603)). Text should not share the colour of the grass behind it ([first logo ever](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-first-logo-ever/318790)).
- **Depth with gradients:** duplicate the logo, darken and desaturate the lower copy, and erase the bottom of each letter on the top copy with a soft brush. This gives a gradient fill that reads better than flat colour ([logo 356982](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-logo/356982)).
- **Sharp output.** "It looks a bit blurry" / "I can barely make out what it is saying" are the most common one-line logo critiques ([game logo](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/opinionfeedback-on-new-game-logo/529185)). Export vector-crisp at final size.
- **A background is not part of a logo.** "I see this a lot in design where the background itself is criticised. It should never be" (younite, [first logo](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-first-logo/452059)). Design the logo to work on transparent, light and dark backgrounds.
- **Originality.** Don't lift text or logos from copyrighted IP. "Taking it from a copyrighted source can lead to you getting termed" ([dragonball logo](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-does-my-dragonball-logo-look/327982)).

### 8.2 Icons and thumbnails
- **Show the game.** "Try to only blur the background a little, so players know what to expect in game and get really excited!" ([logo/icon updated](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-logoicon-updated/472916)). "Add contents on icon/thumbnail that do have a minimum link to the actual gameplay" ([new icon](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-icon-rate-from-1-10/300797)). Accuracy is also a policy requirement (07 §7.11).
- **Clichés work but are forgettable.** A fleeing figure with "REC" text "touches a good majority of users as the message sent visually is clear, but extremely mainstream and cliché". If you use a device such as a camera overlay, commit to it with real camera or glitch effects rather than loose digits ([new icon](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-icon-rate-from-1-10/300797)).
- **Subject separation.** Brighten the character against the background, or the background against a dark-armoured character. Use drop shadows or rim light so hair and fins don't blend into the scene ([game icon](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-game-icon/497954), [game icon 2](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-game-icon-2/498802), [dragonball logo](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-does-my-dragonball-logo-look/327982)).
- **Genre-appropriate lighting.** "I'm guessing this is for a simulator, and simulators use bright lighting" ([game icon 2](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-game-icon-2/498802)). A thumbnail that is too dark hides the characters ([WIP thumbnail](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-looking-for-criticism-on-this-wip-thumbnail/277863)).
- **Remove clutter.** Watermarks and creator names go ([icon](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/opinion-on-my-icon-of-my-new-game/256854)). Replace "Update 3" with a named update or an image of the new feature ([game icon](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-game-icon/497954), [game icon 2](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-game-icon-2/498802)). Don't repeat a label the image already shows ("NEW PET" over a pet).
- **Screenshot-thumbnail recipe (cloudwork):** pose the figures with personality instead of standing "like soldiers"; use non-default lighting and a matching sky (sunset sky for a sunset scene); max graphics for the capture; rule of thirds instead of a centred subject; frame with foreground branches; adjust FOV to dramatise ([screenshot](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-and-ideas-for-my-screenshot/427359)). Give characters a reason to stand where they are, such as looking at or doing something ([WIP thumbnail](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-looking-for-criticism-on-this-wip-thumbnail/277863)). Chapter 02 §15 adds: keep a separate showcase lighting preset for captures.
- **Text on icons:** large, few words, thick outline, high contrast, placed where it doesn't cover the subject. A thin outline at small size pixelates ([thumbnail 778568](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-thumbnail/778568), [group icon](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-wanted-on-group-icon/349718): "Your text is way too small to work for a logo, or icon").
- **Make it look meant.** "It has a presentation of what I like to call 'casual.' Frankly put, it looks sloppy … make your art look like you *meant* it": fuller, straighter outlines and less dead negative space ([group icon](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-wanted-on-group-icon/349718)).
- **Ads (same principles):** halving the words on an ad doubled CTR from 0.6% to 1.2% for one developer. Comic-style ads are suggested. High-CTR ads are often clickbait that doesn't retain players ([CTR ad](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/best-way-to-get-a-high-ctr-on-an-ad/558509)).
- **Expect noise.** In 2025, developers testing thumbnail personalization reported results "almost like it's RNG" and rarely above 0.4% ([QPTR](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-get-qualified-play-through-rate-higher-into-benchmarks/3357473)). Test several genuinely different concepts (subject, colour, framing), not tiny tweaks, and judge on the platform's analytics.

### 8.3 GFX / renders (also applies to in-engine hero shots)
- **Lighting is the #1 skill.** "I cannot stress this enough. Lighting can make your whole scene come to life … If you put an HDRi just because somebody told you … but it doesn't look good, add some custom lighting … dramatic rim lighting" ([GFX course](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-gfx-course-part-1-basics-of-gfx-and-tips/991540)). Use "a couple of colorful area/sun lights behind the character to give it some rim lighting … 3-point lighting" ([improve my GFX](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/in-what-ways-as-a-gfx-artist-improve-my-gfxs/911505)). Use an HDRI of the background so the backdrop actually lights the character ([GFX critique](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/gfx-artist-critque/311513)). A light only at the back with nothing in front leaves the subject too dark, and red-left/blue-right lights must be intentional ([GFX 4](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-gfx-4/497360)). Use a lightroom or Cycles for soft, correct shadows ([GFX work](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/gfx-work-feedback-needed/350379)).
- **Light sources must be physically plausible.** Point lights that seem to sit on the ground with no matching shadows read wrong. Headlights that don't reflect on the ground read wrong ([render 682276](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-rendercriticism-required/682276), [render 690721](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-render-another-render/690721)). Glowing white "void" headlights need an inner LED strip ([GFX 668368](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-gfx/668368)).
- **Pose and story.** "It just doesn't look fitting when the limbs are just straight" ([improve my GFX](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/in-what-ways-as-a-gfx-artist-improve-my-gfxs/911505)). "Make sure your scene tells a story … a happy customer talking to the check in person … a high tension scene where the police and the criminal are in a fight" ([GFX course](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-gfx-course-part-1-basics-of-gfx-and-tips/991540)). Dress and express the avatar for the theme (a smiling avatar next to sad ones reads as an error) ([GFX critique](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/gfx-artist-critque/311513)).
- **Focal point.** "Your background and character is fighting for the spot of which going to be the focus point. Make the character bigger" ([GFX critique](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/gfx-artist-critque/311513)). If plants, fish and rocks are as detailed as the hero, "they draw your eye away from the intended focal point" ([photomanipulation](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-photomanipulation/1003586)). Aim sun-ray streaks *from* a foreground element, not from empty centre-screen ([recent GFX](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-needed-for-my-recent-gfx/359001)). Use light DoF to stop the eye scattering, and the rule of thirds ([personal GFX](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-personal-gfx/516215), [first render](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/my-first-render-feedback-please/305808)).
- **Colour.** A single predominant colour means "everything kind of blends together". Add a contrasting accent ([recent GFX](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-needed-for-my-recent-gfx/359001)). A black shirt on a black background is a classic beginner mistake ([GFX work](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/gfx-work-feedback-needed/350379)).
- **Detail and materials.** "If your scene doesn't look detailed, then people are going to look away from it within seconds" (bump and displacement maps on bricks) ([GFX course](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-gfx-course-part-1-basics-of-gfx-and-tips/991540)). Vary vegetation, because "a regular patch of grass duplicated … with no alteration in scaling, colour, or structure" looks plain ([personal GFX](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-personal-gfx/516215)). Add particles that fit the setting, such as fireflies in a night forest ([first GFX](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-first-gfx/699602)).
- **Compositing hygiene.** No green-screen fringes. Render in 3D rather than screenshot over a blurred photo ([rate my GFX please](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rate-my-gfx-please/328145)). Capes and accessories must cast shadows, glows must not cut off, and colour spill must match ([GFX tips](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/gfx-feedback-and-tips/311669)). Light-wrap the edges so the background light "peeks through the edges of the characters" ([GFX critique](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/gfx-artist-critque/311513)).
- **Post-process.** "Some postprocessing … can go a long way from making your render seem bland and boring, to making it seem interesting." Blender's compositor colour grade counts ([GFX course](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-gfx-course-part-1-basics-of-gfx-and-tips/991540)). Colour theory: hue sets the tone, for example red for aggression or danger and white for holiness (reply, same thread).
- **Ethics and originality.** Don't paste stock or other artists' work as the main subject. Don't use Roblox toolbox models as hero assets. Don't feature well-known creators without permission ([GFX course](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-gfx-course-part-1-basics-of-gfx-and-tips/991540)). For the bot this is absolute: original assets only (CLAUDE.md).

## 11. The bot's critique checklist (run before showing the user anything visual)

**How to run it.** Capture screenshots: at least **4 views** for builds (spawn view at player eye height, landmark vista, one interior, one top-down or three-quarter) and **2 device sizes** for UI (phone ~844×390 and 1080p). Use a mid graphics level, plus one low-level capture for mobile. Look at each screenshot *as a DevForum reviewer would*, and answer every line **Pass / Fail / N/A**. Fix every Fail, re-capture, and re-check. Then report to the user honestly, including what still looks weak. Screenshots are a check, not proof the game is fun (CLAUDE.md: verify by playing).

### 11.1 Build / environment pass

| # | Check | Fail signal | Source pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Style unity**: one shading style (flat *or* smooth), one material generation, one level of detail; no asset looks imported from another game | a smooth-shaded prop in a flat scene; one realistic model; a recognisable toolbox asset | §2.1 #1, [harsh critics](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thorough-and-harsh-critics-needed/245421) |
| B2 | **Lighting not default**: tinted ambient, warm key light (ColorShift_Top) and cool shadows, Atmosphere, subtle CC; settings match 02's preset | grey shadows, stock sky, flat midday | §4 |
| B3 | **No dark holes / every fixture emits**: lamps, windows and screens cast light; no unintended black corners | lamp meshes without lights; unlit shelf areas | [airport](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thoughts-on-my-airport/347308) |
| B4 | **Post-FX restraint**: sun rays not opaque, fog lets you see the far landmark, no BlurEffect, Bloom not haloing everything | glare dominates; haze hides the build | §4.2 |
| B5 | **No dead space**: every room or area has props that tell its purpose; floors not "half-empty"; walls broken up | big empty floors, bare walls, plain ground | §3.2 |
| B6 | **No clutter**: paths and interactables have clear space; ceilings calm; a first-time player knows where to go within 3 s | "sensory overload" | [resort](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thoughts-on-our-resort-build/382794) |
| B7 | **Secondary pass done**: frames, trims, sills, supports, overhangs, roof detail exist | walls are single slabs; windows are flat decals | [first cafe build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-my-first-cafe-build/461349) |
| B8 | **Depth and silhouette**: insets and protrusions, height variation, rooflines; not one flat plane | "boxy", "flat" | §3.5 |
| B9 | **Variation**: ≥ 2–3 variants of any repeated asset (species, size, rotation, colour); ≥ 3 boundary shapes; buildings differ in size and window style | visible copy-paste rhythm | §3.3 |
| B10 | **Imperfection suits the theme**: wear, dirt, lean, uneven boards for old or realistic themes; order and spacing for modern or sci-fi | everything "perfect, clean, safe" in a rustic build, or messy lights in a lab | §3.3 |
| B11 | **Colour**: ≤ 1 dominant hue family plus accents; 2–3 shades per colour; terrain colours match parts; temperature fits meaning | homogeneous or monotone; a "goldy" wash; neon on black | §5 |
| B12 | **Materials**: roads and ground as considered as buildings; no single material on every surface; textures sized right, no visible seams or cut lines | "roads are bland compared to the rest"; carpet texture "cut throughout the middle" | [rate my build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rate-my-build-x10/307800), [quick build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/please-rate-my-quick-build-x10/314554) |
| B13 | **Scale against an R15 dummy**: doors, sinks, counters, stairs (≤ 0.8 rise), windows, railings | dummy looks tiny or huge next to fixtures | §3.4, 02 §0.9 |
| B14 | **Precision**: no z-fighting, gaps, floating parts, clipping; windows centred; one grid | flicker, light leaks, misaligned frames | §3.4 |
| B15 | **Focal point / landmark**: one obvious centrepiece per area, visible from spawn | nothing draws the eye; the tallest object is unfinished | §3.6 |
| B16 | **Uniform level of detail**: the least-detailed visible object is not far below the rest | detailed bed, blocky chairs | §3.5 |
| B17 | **Life**: at least one moving element per area (particles, swaying, flowing water, ambient NPCs) | static diorama | [showcase tips](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-and-showcase-tips-update/639045) |
| B18 | **Something unique**: one memorable feature the genre's other builds lack | "due to the massive amount of cafes out there now, it's getting repetitive" | [this cafe](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thoughts-on-this-cafe/556992) |
| B19 | **Performance sanity**: part, triangle and draw-call budget from 02; unique unions few; decorative collision off; low-graphics capture still readable | budget exceeded; dark or unreadable at low quality | §3.7 |

### 11.5 Presenting the work to the user (mirrors "how to make a quality feedback thread")
- Show **several angles** plus the greybox or "before" shot. List the style spec, palette and LightingStyle preset used.
- State the budget numbers (parts/triangles/draw calls, or texture memory) and the device or graphics level of the screenshots.
- List **known weaknesses** found by this checklist that weren't fixed, and say why.
- Name any third-party or generated asset, so the user knows exactly what is original ([quick build](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/please-rate-my-quick-build-x10/314554), Castlemore: once a free model is spotted, reviewers "can't know how much of the build is *also* constituted by free models").
