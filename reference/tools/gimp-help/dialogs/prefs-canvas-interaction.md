## Zoom

 Zoom

 Drag-to-zoom behavior

 This setting decides how zooming with the
 Zoom tool works. By default,
 the amount of zooming is By distance. The
 farther you drag the zoom rectangle, the larger the amount of
 zooming.

 You can also choose for zooming By duration.
 In this case, the amount of zooming depends on the time spent
 dragging.

 Drag-to-zoom speed

 This adjusts the rate at which dragging the mouse will zoom the
 canvas, as a percentage.
