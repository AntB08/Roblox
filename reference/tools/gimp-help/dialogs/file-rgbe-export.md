## Overview

 The RGBE or
 Radiance HDR image format allows you to export as a
 high dynamic
 range image.
 You can export your image in RGBE format, when you choose the
 .hdr extension, or explicitly choose
 Radiance RGBE as file type in the export dialog.

 There are no settings to choose from. No export dialog will open after
 you have chosen your filename. It will automatically start the export.
