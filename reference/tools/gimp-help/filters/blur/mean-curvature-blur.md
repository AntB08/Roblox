## Overview

## Example for the Mean Curvature Blur filter

Original image

Filter applied

 Regularize geometry at a speed proportional to the local mean curvature 
 value: this filter uses the mean curvature algorithm to blur the image 
 preserving edges.

 The Mean Curvature algorithm is described on Wikipedia.

## Activating the Filter
