## Example for Mark Out of Gamut

Original image

Image with Mark Out Of Gamut and
 Soft Proof enabled in the view menu, and the image's
 soft-proofing profile set to PSO Uncoated v3 (a CMYK profile).

 When enabled, Mark Out Of Gamut Colors will
 mark all pixels that are out of gamut in the current display of your image
 with a specific color that you can set in the
 Soft-Proofing Preferences
 that can be found in the Color Management section.

 This allows you to see which parts of your image will not display
 correctly on your target device (often a printer).

 To get an idea how your image will look enable

 View
 Color Management
 Proof Colors
 .
