## Snap to Bounding Boxes

 Path
 Snap to bounding boxes

 Snap to bounding boxes

 If this option is enabled, moving e.g. layers will snap it to the bounding
 box (edge or center) of other layers. This makes it easy to align them
 horizontally or vertically. Note that this is not restricted to layers,
 you can also use it with e.g. Paths.

## Example of bounding box snapping

 This example shows the red layer snapping horizontally to the center
 of the green layer and vertically to the right edge of the blue layer.
