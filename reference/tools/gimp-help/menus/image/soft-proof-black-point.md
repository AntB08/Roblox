 Black Point Compensation

 When enabled, Black Point Compensation will be
 used for the soft-proof profile when displayed.

 Black Point Compensation is used to compensate for
 differences in black levels available on different devices.
 When enabled, it makes adjustments based on the differences between the
 color profile of the image and that of the output device, as represented
 by the soft-proof profile.

 To get an idea how your image will look with the soft-proof profile
 enable

 View
 Color Management
 Proof Colors
 .
