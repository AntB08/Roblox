## Quadratic 

 

Added in version 4.2.  

The quadratic blending modes are a set of modes intended to give various effects when adding light zones or overlaying shiny objects.  

### Freeze 

The Freeze blending mode. Inversion of the Reflect blending mode.  [img: ../../_images/Blending_modes_Q_Freeze_Light_blue_and_Orange.png]  

Left: **Normal**. Right: **Freeze**.     

### Freeze-Reflect 

Mix of Freeze and Reflect blending modes.  [img: ../../_images/Blending_modes_Q_Freeze_Reflect_Light_blue_and_Orange.png]  

Left: **Normal**. Right: **Freeze-Reflect**.     

### Glow 

The Reflect blending mode with source and destination layers swapped.  [img: ../../_images/Blending_modes_Q_Glow_Light_blue_and_Orange.png]  

Left: **Normal**. Right: **Glow**.     

### Glow-Heat 

Mix of Glow and Heat blending modes.  [img: ../../_images/Blending_modes_Q_Glow_Heat_Light_blue_and_Orange.png]  

Left: **Normal**. Right: **Glow_Heat**.     

### Heat 

The Heat blending mode. Inversion of the Glow blending mode.  [img: ../../_images/Blending_modes_Q_Heat_Light_blue_and_Orange.png]  

Left: **Normal**. Right: **Heat**.     

### Heat-Glow 

Mix of Heat, and Glow blending modes.  [img: ../../_images/Blending_modes_Q_Heat_Glow_Light_blue_and_Orange.png]  

Left: **Normal**. Right: **Heat-Glow**.     

### Heat-Glow and Freeze-Reflect Hybrid 

Mix of the continuous quadratic blending modes. Very similar to Overlay, and sometimes provides better result than Overlay.  [img: ../../_images/Blending_modes_Q_Heat_Glow_Freeze_Reflect_Light_blue_and_Orange.png]  

Left: **Normal**. Right: **Heat-Glow and Freeze-Reflect Hybrid**.     

### Reflect 

Reflect is essentially the Color Dodge blending mode with quadratic falloff.  [img: ../../_images/Blending_modes_Q_Reflect_Light_blue_and_Orange.png]  

Left: **Normal**. Right: **Reflect**.     

### Reflect-Freeze 

Mix of Reflect and Freeze blending modes.  [img: ../../_images/Blending_modes_Q_Reflect_Freeze_Light_blue_and_Orange.png]  

Left: **Normal**. Right: **Reflect-Freeze**.
