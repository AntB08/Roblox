### Experiment Option  Speed

This makes the outputted contour jaggy. The higher the speed, the jaggier.  Smooth

Smoothens the output contour. This slows down the brush, but the higher the smooth, the smoother the contour.  Displace

This displaces the shape. The slow the movement, the higher the displacement and expansion. Fast movements shrink the shape.  Winding Fill

This gives you the option to use a ‘non-zero’ fill rules instead of the ‘even-odd’ fill rule, which means that where normally crossing into the shape created transparent areas, it now will not.  Hard Edge

Removes the anti-aliasing, to get a pixelized line.
