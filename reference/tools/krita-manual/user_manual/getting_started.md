## Getting Started 

Welcome to the Krita Manual! In this section, we’ll try to get you up to speed. 

If you are familiar with digital painting, we recommend checking out the [Introduction Coming From Other Software](introduction_from_other_software.xhtml#introduction-from-other-software) category, which contains guides that will help you get familiar with Krita by comparing its functions to other software. 

If you are new to digital art, just start with [Installation](getting_started/installation.xhtml#installation), which deals with installing Krita, and continue on to [Starting Krita](getting_started/starting_krita.xhtml#starting-with-krita), which helps with making a new document and saving it, [Basic Concepts](getting_started/basic_concepts.xhtml#basic-concepts), in which we’ll try to quickly cover the big categories of Krita’s functionality, and finally, [Navigation](getting_started/navigation.xhtml#navigation), which helps you find basic usage help, such as panning, zooming and rotating. 

When you have mastered those, you can look into the dedicated introduction pages for functionality in the [User Manual](../user_manual.xhtml#user-manual), read through the overarching concepts behind (digital) painting in the [General Concepts](../general_concepts.xhtml#general-concepts) section, or just search the [Reference Manual](../reference_manual.xhtml#reference-manual) for what a specific button does.
